SCENE: A woodland autumn festival in a forest clearing at the edge of a pine wood, and the subject is the vintage carousel standing in the middle of the clearing.

SCENE THEME COLOURS: Three colours carry this picture and they are shared out evenly across it, never piled up. There is no sun disc in the picture, no glow across the sky and no haze: the sky is simply a clean sky blue gradient, deeper at the top and warming to amber only in the last narrow strip above the horizon.

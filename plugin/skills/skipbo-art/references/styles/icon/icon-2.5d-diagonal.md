~~~~text
simplified into 3–5 bold, chunky elements, designed as a casual mobile game icon.

Style: 2D hand-painted mobile game icon with soft volumetric shading (NOT realistic 3D).

Form language: rounded, chunky, and slightly inflated shapes with smooth curves, no sharp angles. Forms are simplified and exaggerated for clarity at small size.

Rendering: clean digital painting with soft gradient shading, but with controlled, slightly defined transitions (not overly airbrushed or blurry).

Lighting: soft directional light with gentle shading to define form, subtle highlights allowed but kept soft and stylized (no realistic reflections or gloss).

Color: bright, saturated colors with gentle hue shifting in shadows (slightly warmer or cooler tones), avoiding pure black. Maintain clean and vibrant color separation.

Edges: subtle colored outlines and edge definition — slightly darker, saturated edge lines to enhance readability (not pure black, not fully lineless).

Occlusion: soft but visible contact shadows where elements overlap, using deeper saturated tones.

Material feel: smooth, stylized, slightly soft surface — closer to painted food illustration than plastic 3D.

Composition: centered, clean, minimal, icon-style , few elements only, balanced and readable. Plain or very subtle background.

Style consistency: matches casual mobile game icons (merge / match-3), semi-flat 2.5D painted style, crisp, polished, and friendly.

Fixed Diagonal View: The object is rotated at roughly 45 degrees relative to the camera, allowing you to see the front/side and the top surfaces simultaneously. This is a classic "2.5D" look used in mobile games to give 2D assets a sense of volume.

Dimensionality: This specific angle is chosen to showcase the icon while still making the top-down shape clearly recognizable.

Focal Length: The lens feels long or orthographic, meaning there is very little perspective distortion or "vanishing point" effect. This keeps the proportions looking consistent and "toy-like" rather than realistic.

Centered Composition: The object is placed dead-center in the frame, angled slightly toward the viewer to create an inviting, "collectible" feel.

--avoid realistic 3D rendering, excessive blur, photorealism, harsh lighting, deep black shadows, complex detail, noise, textures
~~~~

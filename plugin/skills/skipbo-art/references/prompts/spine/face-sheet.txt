ART STYLE (taken from the shipped characters in the style images):
Soft digital painting for a bright casual mobile game, matching the shipped hippo and raccoon exactly. Every shape is a big, chunky, simplified mass with a soft rounded silhouette, slightly swollen and squashy, like a soft toy. Heads and hands are generous in size; hands are fat mittens with three or four short rounded fingers. Interior detail is minimal: a shape reads from its silhouette and its colour.
Shading is smooth airbrushed volume. Each shape has one broad lit side, one broad soft shaded side and a gentle lighter bounce along the shaded edge, blended with smooth gradients. A few soft darker shapes suggest a fold, a pocket or a crease, painted as soft blurred patches. Highlights are broad and soft.
Colour is bright, clean and saturated: candy violet, turquoise, orange, golden yellow, warm mauve. Shadows shift hue toward violet or blue on cool colours and toward red on warm colours, and stay clean and colourful.
Edges are clean and slightly soft. Shapes are separated from each other only by colour and value. There is no outline, contour line or ink line anywhere, and no dark stroke around any shape.
Surfaces are smooth. Fur is shown by a few soft tufts along the silhouette and a gently mottled gradient, never by drawn strands. Cloth, wood and metal are smooth painted colour with a soft highlight, with no grain, weave or texture.
Eyes are simple glossy dark ovals with one small white highlight, set into soft eye areas; noses and nostrils are simple soft dark shapes.

THE PIECES (nine heads, each painted exactly once), laid out in a three by three grid, left to right and top to bottom:
Each head is the character's head base from image_1, front view, with its eyes, eyebrows, snout or beak, nostrils and mouth, and without any hat, horn, antler, ear or hair tuft, because those are separate pieces. All nine heads have exactly the same outline, size and fur, skin or feathers; only the eyes, eyebrows and mouth change, and they stay the same small size they have on the head in image_1.
1. Neutral: eyes open, eyebrows relaxed, gentle closed smile, exactly as in image_1.
2. Blink: the same face with both eyes closed as gentle curved eyelid lines.
3. Happy: eyes open and bright, wide open smiling mouth with a dark-red inside and a pink tongue.
4. Laugh: eyes squeezed shut in happy upward curves, wide open laughing mouth.
5. Surprised: eyes wide open, eyebrows raised high, small round open mouth.
6. Sad: eyebrows tilted up in the middle, eyes open and looking down, gentle downturned mouth.
7. Angry: eyebrows pulled down in the middle, eyes narrowed, mouth a tight frown.
8. Talking: eyes open, mouth half open in a relaxed oval as in mid-word.
9. Wink: the left eye closed in a gentle curve, the right eye open, a cheeky smile.

All pieces are laid out on one flat plain light grey background. Every piece stands apart from every other piece with a clear gap of empty background around it, at least as wide as one of the character's fingers. Pieces never touch, never overlap, and stay fully inside the frame. Each piece is shown flat, facing the viewer, upright, and all pieces share one scale. The sheet contains only these nine heads, painted fully opaque, with no faint, ghosted or half-transparent shapes anywhere, and with no labels, numbers, text, guides or shadows.

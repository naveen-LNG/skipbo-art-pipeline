# Character: Moose (Tiles2611 host, grandpa train conductor)

- **Design ref:** `references/characters/moose/moose_turn_00006_.png` (in the `comfy/` repo, not in this skill)
- **Approved Blocks master:** (repo) `experiments/2026-10-11-moose-parts/runs/e2e-moose-v2/*_n41_00.png` (2026-10-11)

## CHARACTER

~~~~text
A kind grandpa moose train conductor: dark-brown fur, a big rounded tan muzzle with two large dark nostrils, small black eyes, bushy cream eyebrows, round ears and broad flat antlers. Navy conductor cap with a gold band and a round gold badge, red scarf with a small gold bell, navy vest with three gold buttons and a gold belt buckle over a cream long-sleeved shirt, brown trousers, dark-brown hoof hands and hooves.
~~~~

## PIECES (body sheet v2, 20 pieces)

~~~~text
Row 1: head base (the furry head with the big tan muzzle and its two nostrils, plain fur forehead and eye areas, plain muzzle below the nostrils), conductor cap, left antler, right antler, left ear, right ear.
Row 2: red scarf with its knot and hanging end, small gold bell, torso (the navy vest with its buttons and buckle over the cream shirt, continuing up into a short brown neck and ending at the gold belt buckle; both shoulders are rounded cream shirt, and the cream shirt sides are painted down to the waist; no trousers, legs or hooves on it), hips (only the top of the brown trousers, from the belt down to the crotch, painted whole, with two rounded leg openings).
Row 3: four separate sleeve pieces, each painted upright: left upper arm, right upper arm (the upper half of the cream sleeve only, from shoulder to elbow, rounded at both ends, no cuff), left forearm, right forearm (the lower half of the cream sleeve only, from elbow to wrist, rounded at the top, with the brown fur wrist at the bottom); then left hand, right hand (dark-brown hoof mittens, open and relaxed, rounded at the wrist).
Row 4: left leg, right leg (one brown trouser leg each, from the crotch down to its hem, rounded at the top, with nothing below the hem), left hoof, right hoof (only the dark-brown hoof that shows below the trouser hem, with a rounded top and no trouser on it).
~~~~

The Moose took three body-sheet passes. The long trousers hid where leg and hoof split, so the torso ends "at the gold belt buckle" and the leg ends "at its hem". The four sleeve pieces needed the explicit Row 3 wording above. The final rig uses the arms from pass 3 and every other piece from pass 2 (pass 3 joined the cap to the head). Both sheets were painted at the same scale: compare torso widths before mixing sheets.

## HANDS (hands sheet)

~~~~text
relaxed (exactly the hoof hand of image_1, open and relaxed); open (palm facing the viewer, all fingers spread wide, as in a wave); fist (fingers curled into a soft round fist); point (index finger stretched straight down, the other fingers curled); thumbs-up (fingers curled into a fist, thumb sticking straight out sideways, away from the body); grip (fingers curled loosely forward and the thumb pressed against the side of the first finger, as when pinching something thin).
~~~~

## POSES (poses sheet)

~~~~text
Row 1: left upper arm, left upper arm raised, right upper arm, right upper arm raised. An upper arm is only the cream sleeve from the shoulder down to the elbow, rounded at both ends, without any cuff or fur wrist (those belong to the forearm). A raised upper arm is the same sleeve at the same size and width as it looks on an arm lifted high above the head, but painted upright like the first one, shoulder end at the top: its shoulder end is a soft, slightly wider rounded cap with one or two gentle folds where the cloth bunches.
Row 2: left hoof, left hoof on tiptoe, right hoof, right hoof on tiptoe (only the dark-brown hoof that shows below the trouser hem, with no trouser on it; on tiptoe the hoof is seen from the front with its tip pointing down and its sole tilted towards the viewer, as when jumping, with its rounded ankle end still at the top).
~~~~

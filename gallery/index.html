How to review

Side by side plays today's pipeline (Legacy) on the left of each cell and the new Pro rig on the right, on one shared clock. Start with idle, walk, wave and cheer_jump: that is where the tail follow-through, ear flop, breathing and planted feet show. look_around and talk are new and Pro only.

Pipeline problems found (your call)

  • Tiptoe scaling bug. The tiptoe variant is sized against the rest foot's unsnapped width; the bear's clogs came out 3.2× tall. spine_qa passed it; the gallery caught it.
  • Scale from neck-to-waist only. Chunky bodies come out too wide, and one bad waist point shrank the rabbit to IoU 0.64, with no doubt raised.
  • Master contract misses pale fur. Light-grey fur and white muzzles on the grey background key out as holes.
  • One-piece trousers. The body sheet's "each leg in two" fights them; all three characters had trousers rebuilt by a local script.
  • Sleeves and snaps. The sleeve prompt can't say "rolled up over a bare forearm"; point snapping fails on long sleeve caps, clog heels and slanted mittens.

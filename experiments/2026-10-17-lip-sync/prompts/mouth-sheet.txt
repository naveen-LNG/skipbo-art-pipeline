THE PIECES (nine heads, each painted exactly once), laid out in a three by three grid, left to right and top to bottom:
1. Rest: the mouth exactly as in image_1, lips closed in a relaxed line, the two big front teeth resting on the lower lip.
2. M B P: lips pressed firmly together in a flat closed line, a little tighter and shorter than at rest, the front teeth still showing above the lips.
3. Consonant: the mouth only slightly open, the jaw almost shut, a narrow dark gap between the front teeth and the lower lip, as when saying "s", "t" or "ee"; lips stretched a little wider.
4. Open: the mouth open about as tall as the front teeth are long, a dark-red inside and the tip of a pink tongue, as when saying "eh".
5. Wide open: the jaw dropped, the mouth open its widest and tallest, a dark-red inside and a pink tongue, as when saying "ah". The open mouth stays inside the cream muzzle.
6. Round: the mouth slightly rounded and open, a small soft oval with a dark-red inside, as when saying "oh" or "er".
7. Pucker: the lips pushed forward into a small tight round "oo" shape with a tiny dark opening, as when saying "oo" or "w"; the front teeth hidden behind the round lips.
8. F V: the two big front teeth pressing down on the lower lip, the lower lip tucked under them, the mouth corners closed, as when saying "f" or "v".
9. L: the mouth open about as much as "eh", with the pink tongue tip raised and touching behind the front teeth, as when saying "l".

A living room in a home-makeover game. Its three theme colours are a dominant warm honey brown, carried by the plank floor and all the woodwork; a supporting clean mid teal, carried by the striped back wall; and a paler warm cream, carried by the ceiling, the plasterwork and the daylight at the window.

THE CAMERA: one fixed camera standing inside the room, level and not tilted, in a tall vertical nine by sixteen frame that takes in the ceiling along its top edge and a wide sweep of floor along its bottom edge. The eye line crosses the frame about one fifth of the way down from the top, just above the head of the arched window. Everything above that line is seen a little from below and shows its underside: the ceiling, and the hanging lamp, whose shades show their open undersides. Everything below that line is seen a little from above and shows its top surfaces: the fireplace's mantelshelf, the window sill, the seat of the sofa, and the whole floor, which opens out widely towards the bottom of the frame.

THE WALLS AND THE FLOOR: the back wall stands square to the camera and carries the arched window on its left half; its vertical stripes run straight up and down and do not converge. The right hand wall recedes away to the right and carries the fireplace's projecting chimney breast, so that breast turns its left hand face towards the camera and towards the window. The floor is wide planks laid diagonally, running from the lower left of the frame away towards the upper right, their long seams converging very gently as they go.

THE LIGHT: one key light only, the daylight that comes in through the arched window on the back wall at the upper left. Every object in the room carries its broad lit passage on its upper left side and falls into soft shade on its lower right side, and each one lays a single soft shadow on the floor that is darkest and tightest where it touches down and spreads a short way towards its lower right before fading out altogether. Those shadows are a deeper, warmer brown of the planks themselves and never a grey. The room reads light everywhere, with no dark passages and no strong contrast.

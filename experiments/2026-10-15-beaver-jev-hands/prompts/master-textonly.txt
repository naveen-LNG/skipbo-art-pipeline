ART STYLE (taken from the shipped characters in the style images):
Soft digital painting for a bright casual mobile game, matching the shipped hippo and raccoon exactly. Every shape is a big, chunky, simplified mass with a soft rounded silhouette, slightly swollen and squashy, like a soft toy. Heads and hands are generous in size; hands are fat mittens with three or four short rounded fingers. Interior detail is minimal: a shape reads from its silhouette and its colour.
Shading is smooth airbrushed volume. Each shape has one broad lit side, one broad soft shaded side and a gentle lighter bounce along the shaded edge, blended with smooth gradients. A few soft darker shapes suggest a fold, a pocket or a crease, painted as soft blurred patches. Highlights are broad and soft.
Colour is bright, clean and saturated: candy violet, turquoise, orange, golden yellow, warm mauve. Shadows shift hue toward violet or blue on cool colours and toward red on warm colours, and stay clean and colourful.
Edges are clean and slightly soft. Shapes are separated from each other only by colour and value. There is no outline, contour line or ink line anywhere, and no dark stroke around any shape.
Surfaces are smooth. Fur is shown by a few soft tufts along the silhouette and a gently mottled gradient, never by drawn strands. Cloth, wood and metal are smooth painted colour with a soft highlight, with no grain, weave or texture.
Eyes are simple glossy dark ovals with one small white highlight, set into soft eye areas; noses and nostrils are simple soft dark shapes.

POSE AND FRAMING:
Front view, facing the viewer, in a symmetrical rest pose. Both arms hang down and out at about thirty degrees from the sides, so plain background shows between each arm and the body along the whole arm. Hands open and relaxed. Legs straight, feet a little apart and flat on the ground. A tail, if the character has one, hangs to one side and stays clear of the legs, with background visible between them. Head level, looking at the viewer, with a gentle closed-mouth smile and eyes open. The whole character fits in the frame with an even margin around it and fills about eighty-five percent of its height. The background is one flat plain light grey with nothing else in it, and the character stands on it without any cast shadow.
